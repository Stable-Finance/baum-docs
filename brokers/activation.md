# Activation

A Broker must be activated to earn. Activation authorizes a Baum instance on the Broker's wallet and pays a one-time fee in BAUM.

| Broker level | Activation fee |
| ------------ | -------------- |
| Level 1 – 2  | 50,000 BAUM    |
| Level 3 – 5  | 100,000 BAUM   |

These fees halve as BAUM's FDV grows — see [Sunset rule](../points-and-levels/sunset-rule.md).

## Where the fee goes

* **50%** is burned.
* **50%** goes to the Baum treasury.

## Transfers

Activation clears whenever the Broker changes hands; the new owner must reactivate.

The Broker's level and Multiplier Attribute do **not** clear — they belong to the NFT.
