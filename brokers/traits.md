# Sub-DAOs, traits and the Multiplier Attribute

## Sub-DAO membership

Each Broker is assigned at claim — at random and with equal weight — to one of **five sub-DAOs**. Each sub-DAO is an asset class. **Stocks** is the first; the other four are named at reveal. See [Sub-DAOs](../sub-daos/README.md).

## Traits

Every Broker carries seven traits:

1. Background
2. Body
3. Hair
4. Eyewear
5. Prop
6. Outfit
7. Multiplier Attribute

A Broker's rarity comes from its whole trait set, and a rare Broker's premium is what collectors pay for those traits.

Every Broker is a playing card in the Deed Deck. Art and traits are revealed the week of **September 28**.

## Multiplier Attribute

The Multiplier Attribute is the one trait with a mechanical effect: **it multiplies the points the Broker earns, and nothing else.** It does not change reward weight, allocations, or governance.

| Multiplier Attribute | Share (base odds) | Points multiplier |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Common               | \~85%             | ×1.00             |
| Uncommon             | \~12%             | ×1.25             |
| Rare                 | \~3%              | ×1.50             |

A lucky roll climbs the levels faster, but every Broker can reach Level 5. The multiplier is applied after the daily points cap — see [Earning points](../points-and-levels/README.md#daily-cap).

Reserving 3 or 5 Brokers in one commit improves these odds — see [Mint](mint.md#reserving-in-quantity).
