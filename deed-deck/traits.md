# Cards and traits

## The deck

The Deed Deck is a full deck of cards: the 52 standard cards plus two jokers, for **54 faces**. Each face is printed **72 times**, so 54 × 72 = 3,888 Brokers.

Every card belongs to one of four types:

| Type   | Cards   | What's on it |
| ------ | ------- | ------------ |
| Title  | Aces    | A house on its suit's street |
| Block  | 2 – 10  | The houses on its suit's street |
| People | J, Q, K | The people who move a deal: Jacks are brokers, Queens are listing agents, Kings are lenders |
| Chaos  | Jokers  | The jester |

Each suit is a street:

| Suit     | Street         |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Hearts   | 19 Magnolia    |
| Diamonds | 1847 Palisades |
| Clubs    | 12 Ironwood    |
| Spades   | 4400 Balboa    |

On People cards, red suits (Hearts and Diamonds) and black suits (Clubs and Spades) each have their own character for every rank.

Art and traits are revealed the week of **September 28**.

## Traits

A Broker's rarity comes from its whole trait set, and a rare Broker's premium is what collectors pay for those traits. These traits are cosmetic — only the [Multiplier Attribute](#multiplier-attribute) affects earnings.

## Multiplier Attribute

The Multiplier Attribute is the one trait with a mechanical effect: **it multiplies the points the Broker earns, and nothing else.** It does not change reward weight, allocations, or governance.

| Multiplier Attribute | Share (base odds) | Points multiplier |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Common               | \~85%             | ×1.00             |
| Uncommon             | \~12%             | ×1.25             |
| Rare                 | \~3%              | ×1.50             |

A lucky roll climbs the levels faster, but every Broker can reach Level 5. The multiplier is applied after the daily points cap — see [Earning points](../points-and-levels/README.md#daily-cap).

Reserving 3 or 5 Brokers in one commit improves these odds — see [Mint](mint.md#reserving-in-quantity).

## Sub-DAO membership

Each Broker is assigned at claim — at random and with equal weight — to one of **five sub-DAOs**. Each sub-DAO is an asset class. **Stocks** is the first; the other four are named at reveal. See [Sub-DAOs](../sub-daos/README.md).
