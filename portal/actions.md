# Portal actions

{% hint style="info" %}
**Not live yet.** Today Baum works through the chat — swaps on Solana and Robinhood Chain, and alerts on the traders and members of Congress you follow. See [Messaging Baum](../quickstart.md#messaging-baum).

The actions below open to all Deed Deck holders when beta opens, around October 1. See [Schedule](../schedule.md).
{% endhint %}

## When beta opens

1. **Hold** USDX in the Broker wallet.
2. **LP** a core pool:
   * USDX/USDG
   * mUSDX/USDX
   * BAUM/USDX
   * ETH/USDX
3. **Swap** through USDX — BAUM, Stock Tokens, sub-DAO tokens — as a one-off, a DCA schedule, or a limit order.
4. **Lend** USDG to the [USDX lending market](../lending-market.md).

## Coming later

Added as they go live:

* LP in RWA pools
* Arbitrage
* Yield optimization across every asset in the wallet
* Borrowing USDG against USDX
* Perps
* Sub-DAO products

See [Earning points](../points-and-levels/README.md) for how each action earns.
