# Yield beyond USDX

Baum's job in a Broker wallet is to earn. USDX is the base, not the boundary.

As tokenized stocks, commodities and other real-world assets list on Robinhood Chain, Baum provides liquidity to their pools paired with USDX, and arbitrages between pools and venues when prices drift. The owner sets the assets and the limits; Baum finds the yield and moves the capital, every hour, without being asked.

## Robinhood account connection

The Robinhood account connection extends the same job off-chain. It is designed to be thoughtless: connect once through Robinhood's agentic trading tools, and the Baum instance already operating the Broker wallet acts in the brokerage account under the same limits — with no separate setup, strategy or configuration.

**One agent, one set of rules, both accounts.**
