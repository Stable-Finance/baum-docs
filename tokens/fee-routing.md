# Fee routing

Baum earns:

* Portal fees (see [Portal fees](../portal/fees.md))
* LP fees
* USDX/mUSDX arbitrage

All fees are collected in **USDX**.

## Routing

| Phase | Operations | Buybacks | Liquidity & lending capital |
| ----- | ---------- | -------- | --------------------------- |
| Until 12 months of operating runway is banked | 70% | — | 30% to liquidity |
| After that | 10% | 20% | 70% to USDX/mUSDX liquidity, arbitrage, and USDX lending capital |
