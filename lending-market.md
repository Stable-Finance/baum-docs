# USDX lending market

A single Morpho-style market on Robinhood Chain, network-wide:

* **Collateral:** USDX
* **Loans:** USDG
* **Rate:** standard utilization-kinked interest rate

Borrowed USDG mints USDX, so lending capital flows into USDX reserves and the USDX supply grows with the market.

## Lenders earn three ways

1. Market interest from borrowers
2. BAUM incentives on lent USDG, at a rate set when the market opens
3. Broker points at the highest rate in the system — 3 pts per $1 per day (see [Earning points](points-and-levels/README.md))

## Initial targets

* $20M USDG lent
* $20M USDX supply
