**No one from Baum will ever DM you for your seed phrase or private key**, or ask you to send funds to "verify", "sync" or "claim" anything.
{% endhint %}

* Mint only at [baumai.xyz](https://baumai.xyz) or from the [OpenSea collection](https://opensea.io/collection/baum-deed-deck) linked above.
