# Sunset rule

Activation fees and level locks halve automatically and permanently at three FDV thresholds:

|                   | Launch       | At $25M FDV | At $75M FDV | At $225M FDV |
| ----------------- | ------------ | ----------- | ----------- | ------------ |
| Activation, L1–2  | 50,000 BAUM  | 25,000      | 12,500      | 6,250        |
| Activation, L3–5  | 100,000 BAUM | 50,000      | 25,000      | 12,500       |
| Level 4 lock      | 100,000 BAUM | 50,000      | 25,000      | 12,500       |
| Level 5 lock      | 250,000 BAUM | 125,000     | 62,500      | 31,250       |

See [Activation](../deed-deck/activation.md) and [Levels](levels.md).
