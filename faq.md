# FAQ

<details>

<summary>What is Baum?</summary>

Baum is the AI agent that runs inside Stable's lender portal. It reviews mortgage documents, moves loans onto the Stable Mortgage Registry, and keeps USDX liquid and on peg. It's now opening up to run individual onchain accounts too. See [Introduction](README.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the Deed Deck? What's a Broker?</summary>

The Baum Deed Deck is a collection of 3,888 playing-card NFTs. Each card is a Broker, and each Broker has its own onchain wallet that Baum can run for you. See [The Deed Deck](deed-deck/README.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I get a Broker?</summary>

Mint one in the public mint for 0.03 ETH at [baumai.xyz](https://baumai.xyz), or buy one on [OpenSea](https://opensea.io/collection/baum-deed-deck). See [Quickstart](quickstart.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>When can I use Baum?</summary>

Baum is in private beta now. Beta opens to all Deed Deck holders the week of September 28.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does Baum hold my funds?</summary>

No. Your Broker's funds sit in its own wallet, and you own the Broker. Baum can only use approved contracts, stays within a maximum slippage, and can't send funds anywhere except back to you. See [Portal overview](portal/README.md#scoped-authorization).

On Telegram, Baum can also make you a trading wallet on each chain it trades. It gives you the private key to each one and then deletes that message, so save the key somewhere safe. Baum only uses these wallets for swaps you confirm. See [Quickstart](quickstart.md#wallets).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why isn't my Broker earning?</summary>

A Broker has to be [activated](deed-deck/activation.md) to earn. Activation resets whenever a Broker changes hands, so if you just bought one, activate it again.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens when I sell my Broker?</summary>

Its level, traits and wallet contents go with it to the buyer. Activation resets. Withdraw anything you want to keep first.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I earn more by adding more money to one Broker?</summary>

Only up to the daily cap of 30,000 points (about $10,000 of USDG lent). Past that, the way to earn more is more Brokers. See [Earning points](points-and-levels/README.md#daily-cap).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does the Multiplier Attribute do?</summary>

It multiplies the points a Broker earns — ×1.25 for Uncommon, ×1.5 for Rare — and nothing else. See [Traits](deed-deck/traits.md#multiplier-attribute).

</details>

<details>

<summary>When are the traits revealed?</summary>

The week of September 28. Until then every Broker shows the same unrevealed card.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Was there a private sale of BAUM?</summary>

No. Baum raises money by adding BAUM liquidity at set price levels on the open market. See [Treasury LP-out ladder](tokens/treasury-lp-out-ladder.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Where are the contract addresses?</summary>

On [Contracts](contracts.md). Only trust addresses listed there or at [baumai.xyz](https://baumai.xyz).

</details>
